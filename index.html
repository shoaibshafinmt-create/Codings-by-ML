🇵🇰 Our Story

The Birth of a Nation

On 14 August 1947, Pakistan emerged as an independent nation — a dream realised through unwavering determination and sacrifice.

14 August is celebrated as Pakistan's Independence Day. On this historic day in 1947, Pakistan emerged as an independent nation, carving a new destiny on the world map.

Independence Day is a time to remember the struggle, determination, and sacrifices of those who worked for freedom. It is also a reminder that we all have a role in building a peaceful, prosperous, and united Pakistan.

From the visionary leadership of Quaid-e-Azam to the resilience of millions, the story of Pakistan is one of courage, faith, and relentless hope.

Key Milestones

The road to independence was paved with vision, struggle, and unwavering commitment.

1940

Lahore Resolution

The demand for a separate homeland for Muslims of the subcontinent is formally made.

1947

Independence Day

Pakistan is born on 14 August 1947, becoming the world's first Islamic republic.

1956

Islamic Republic

Pakistan adopts its constitution, officially becoming the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.
